Two-robot collaborative SLAM: clean merged maps & item-search benchmark

Overnight autonomous session 2026-07-13 → 07-14 · office_world / depot_world · 8 sim runs (RTF capped 1.0, GPU-rendered sensors, headless), zero explorer errors.

Headline findings
  1. The doubled walls in every earlier merged map were not SLAM drift. The rovers' /odom comes from Gazebo's world-frame OdometryPublisher, so each robot's SLAM frame is already world-anchored — the hard-coded (1.5 m, 0) spawn-offset in the static TFs and compositor double-shifted leo2's submap. Occupancy-grid registration exposed this: seeded at (1.5, 0) it converged to (−0.055, 0.000, 0.35°).
  2. Merged maps are now report-ready: wall IoU@10cm 0.78–0.80, precision ≈0.99, RMSE-to-true-wall ≈4–5 cm on both worlds — matching single-robot map quality.
  3. Coordinated 2-robot item search decisively beats both baselines: 15/16 markers found across two layouts vs 9/16 (independent) and 8/16 (single); 2–4× faster to 4 items.

1 · Report-ready merged maps (Priority 1)

New offline pipeline (scripts/map_fusion.py, also run live by the compositor): multi-resolution correlative registration on a likelihood field of occupied cells → per-cell log-odds fusion (with weak free votes beside a map's own walls, so sub-cell wall edges cannot punch holes in the other map's walls) → small-component despeckle, unknown-hole fill, world-bound clip. Scored against the world SDF rasterized at the lidar plane (scripts/world_ground_truth.py).

Before = the old fixed-offset overwrite merge (doubled partitions, ghost desks and barrels 1.5 m apart). After = registered + fused + cleaned, from the same per-robot maps.

2 · Registration & the relaxed prior (Priorities 1 + 3)

Synthetic ground truth (a real office map split into two overlapping halves, one displaced by a known transform): with an exact prior (seed ≤0.3 m/3° off) recovery error is 0.011 m / 0.10° mean; with a coarse prior (seed up to 1.14 m / 11.6° off, the Priority-3 assumption) it is 0.015 m / 0.11° mean, 0.061 m / 0.34° max (n=8 each). On the real pair, 5/5 random coarse seeds (±1 m, ±15°) converge to within 0.01 m / 0.15° of the exact-window answer — the known-spawn assumption is not needed, only overlapping coverage and a rough prior.

3 · Item-search benchmark (Priority 2)

Multi-robot port of the PR4 item search: per-robot mock ArUco detectors (own-frame, LOS on the robot's own map, markers shifted common→own via TF), a shared item registry on /item_claims (peers adopt confirmed items and skip redundant verification detours), and shared camera-coverage claims every 3 s (no wall is swept twice; sweep targets near a peer's active claim are skipped). Conditions: 1robot, 2indep (no sharing, no coordination), 2coord (allocation + sharing). office_world, 8 wall markers, two layouts (seed A/B), 20-minute sim cap, cameras on.

Honest limitations

  • n=2 marker layouts per condition; sim run-to-run variance is ~±30% for coverage-style metrics. The coordinated gap exceeds 2× on both seeds, but two seeds cannot fully rule out layout luck.
  • Independent and single runs hit the cap with items missing — their times are right-censored lower bounds; found-counts are counts-at-cap. The cap applies equally to all conditions.
  • Mock geometric ArUco detector (range/FOV/incidence/LOS gating), not real perception.
  • Registration assumes zero relative yaw between map frames (true for this stack) and overlapping coverage; seeds vary marker layout only, spawns fixed.
  • RTF cap changed 2.0→1.0 this session for control fidelity; absolute times are not comparable with earlier reports.
